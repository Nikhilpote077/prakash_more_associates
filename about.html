OUR STORY

A Studio Built on Precision, Patience, and Purpose

Prakash More & Associates was founded with a simple belief: architecture should serve the people who live in it. For over 17 years, our studio has designed and delivered homes, villas, and commercial interiors across Goa — each one shaped by close collaboration with our clients, careful attention to local context, and a deep respect for Vastu principles.

What began as a small architectural practice in Mapusa has grown into a full-service design studio, handling everything from initial concept sketches and Vastu-aligned floor plans to final interior styling and handover — without ever losing the personal, hands-on approach we started with.

Every project we take on is treated as a long-term relationship, not a transaction. We believe the best spaces come from genuinely understanding how our clients want to live and work.

Principal Architect

Prakash More

Prakash More leads the studio's architectural and Vastu planning work, bringing 17+ years of hands-on experience designing residential and commercial spaces across North Goa. His approach blends contemporary architectural thinking with authentic Vastu Shastra principles, ensuring every space is both functionally sound and energetically balanced.

Prakash personally oversees every project from the first consultation through to final handover, working closely with a trusted network of contractors, craftsmen, and interior specialists to ensure design intent is preserved through execution.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

How We Think About Design

Contextual Integration

Every design begins with the site itself — climate, natural light, and surrounding landscape shape our architectural decisions from day one.

Material Authenticity

We favor high-grade natural stone, warm woods, and sustainable finishes that age gracefully rather than trend-driven materials that date quickly.

Uncompromising Precision

Rigorous attention to joinery, lighting geometry, and spatial proportions — the details that separate a good space from a great one.

Vastu Harmony

Authentic Vastu Shastra principles are woven into planning from the earliest stage, not applied as an afterthought.

Functional Luxury

Beautiful spaces should also work hard — every layout is tested against how our clients actually live day to day.

Honest Communication

Clear timelines, transparent costs, and regular updates throughout design and execution — no surprises.
